Your learning progress

How change is calculated: Post-test percentage minus pre-test percentage. The result is measured in percentage points, not percentage growth. For example, 60% to 80% is an increase of 20 percentage points.

Privacy and interpretation

This calculator runs entirely in your browser. It does not collect, send, save or retrieve your scores. Your entries are not submitted to Canvas. If you print or save the page, take care when sharing personal learning information.

The programme's evaluation objective is to investigate mean change of 15 percentage points across matched learners, where appropriate baseline data are available. That is a group evaluation target, not a required result for an individual learner. The calculator is for personal reflection and does not affect Marks.
